The publication

A news site run by a teenager in Pennsylvania.

The Uncomanor was an American news publication based in Pennsylvania. It was founded in April 2020 by Gabe Sullivan, a teenage political enthusiast, and published news, analysis, opinion, satire, and political cartoons until late 2022.

At its height the site had four contributors and covered the 2020 election, the coronavirus pandemic, Congress, the tech industry, and cryptocurrency. It was viewed more than 120,000 times.

The original site is offline. This page collects its history, its people, and a few of the stories it published.

The history

A short history of a short paper

From a domain name to a newsroom to an archive, in six entries.

  1. April 2020

    A domain and an idea

    Gabe Sullivan, a teen political enthusiast in Pennsylvania, starts The Uncomanor. The first coverage is consumed by the two stories of the year: the 2020 election and the coronavirus pandemic.

  2. May 2020

    First impressions

    The earliest archived homepage offers “A Newsletter of Our Best Stories,” a COVID-era news feed, a section called Cartoons, and a promise of “quality video, article, and podcast content.” It is unmistakably a website made in 2020.

  3. 2020

    The newsroom

    The masthead grows to four: Gabe as editor-in-chief; Jenna DeVinney on politics; Michael Pagano on pop culture; and Kyler Stigelman on pop culture and tech. The About page states the creed plainly: “We write what is right.”

  4. 2021

    Everything, all at once

    News, analysis, opinion, humor, satire, cartoons, interactive polls, a Political Spectrum Test, a career quiz, media-bias ratings, and a newsletter called Down the Rabbit Hole. The paper discovers that a website can have as many sections as it wants.

  5. January 2022

    The pivot

    A redesign narrows the paper to News, Analysis, Politics, The Internet, and Crypto. There are shopping guides, a sister site called Urupto, and a privacy policy updated on January 16. The homepage has never looked more certain of itself.

  6. November 2022

    The last update

    The final archived homepage opens, “Good afternoon. Here’s your news briefing,” with a story about Twitter under its new owner. Then the updates stop. The domain stays registered, and the archive stays up.

The people

The masthead

  • Gabe Sullivan

    Founder & Editor-in-Chief

    Politics enthusiast and host of If It Interests You, a YouTube channel about economics, business, and all things interesting.

  • Jenna DeVinney

    Politics Opinion Contributor

    Wrote op-eds focused on politics, including a case for abolishing the age requirements for the presidency.

  • Michael Pagano

    Pop Culture Opinion Contributor

    Wrote about pop culture, including whether downloadable content makes or breaks the games we love.

  • Kyler Stigelman

    Pop-Culture & Tech Reporter

    Covered pop culture and the tech industry. A lover of video games.
